## Key Findings

1. **Volume signals dominate** — source_count, doc_count, burst_ratio, velocity_windowed are the top predictors. Narrative attention → price momentum.
2. **Sentiment variance > sentiment mean** — Division is more predictive than consensus direction. Strong volatility indicator.
3. **Cross-source score is contrarian** — Source agreement today → price falls tomorrow. Consistent with "crowded trade" dynamics.
4. **NS Score underperforms its inputs** — The composite (0.284) is less predictive than source_count, burst_ratio, doc_count, entropy, or velocity_windowed individually. Aggregation formula may be washing out signal.
5. **Windowed velocity >> raw velocity** — Recency-weighting (0.439 vs 0.294) provides significant predictive improvement.

## Caveats

- 8 pairs, 11-15 data points each — directional evidence, not statistically robust.
- Mixed asset classes (crypto-adjacent tickers like SVXY, GDX vs traditional like ITW, AIG).
- r_lead measures one-day-ahead correlation only.
- More pipeline cycles needed to confirm these patterns.
